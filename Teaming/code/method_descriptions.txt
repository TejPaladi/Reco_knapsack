USE CASE 1 DETAILS:

M0 - Random Matching
Given a researcher's name, show a list of randomly selected proposals and candidate teams, irrespective of relevance or any skillset requirements.

M1 - String Matching
Given a researcher's name, use string matching to select proposals and form candidate teams. If a proposal's requirements are directly matched (or within a threshold) with a researcher's interests, then that proposal is deemed to be a good fit for the researcher. Similarly, gather candidate team members and form teams by matching their interests with the ones mentioned in the proposal.

M2 - Mapper Matching
Given a researcher's name, use a ACM/JEL's ontology classification guide to select proposals and form candidate teams. Unlike methods such as string matching, mapper matching also allows for semantic meanings to take place. (E.g., our mapper recognizes that "machine learning" and "language models" are similar in domains.)
